For the final project, I plan on creating a sudoku solver as well as a sudoku 
puzzle generator. The solving algorithm will be a mix of backtracking and human 
strategies such as naked single and hidden single. The generator will start from
a randomized filled puzzle and remove digits while retaining a unique solution.

Milestones
Easy
- Basic puzzle representation
- Verifier
- Naked single 
- Hidden single

Medium
- Backtracking
- TUI (using brick library?)

Challenge
- Puzzle generator with seed
