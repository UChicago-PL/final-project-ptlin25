For the final project, I plan on creating a sudoku solver as well as a sudoku 
puzzle generator. The sudoku solver will mimic human/logic solving techniques like
hidden/naked singles, hidden/naked pairs, box-line reduction, and X-wing. I will
also implement a backtracking algorithmic solver to aid in puzzle generation. 
The puzzle generator will start from a randomly filled puzzle. At each step, a
clue will be removed. If the puzzle retains a unique solution (verified with
the backtracking algorithm), the logic solver will be used to check that the
puzzle is solvable. If the puzzle fails this check, the clue will be reintroduced.
